# Bio image-to-3D generation brief (character-preserving)

## Input
Use the four crops generated by `scripts/prepare_refs.py`:
`front.png`, `three_quarter.png`, `side.png`, `back.png`.
The images are original art from the creator, **not** four exact orthographic captures.
Crop away labels and backgrounds only; do not ask image generation to redraw the mascot before multi-view reconstruction.

## Primary objective
Create a **consistent, fully three-dimensional, editable chibi robot mascot** matching the four references rather than inventing a new cartoon robot. The character is **Bio / 拜爾** and wears a yellow engineering hard hat, lime-green protective armor, green helmet leaf badge, large charcoal green face display, lime LED eyes and a green/white chest gas gauge.

## Shape-critical requirements
- Wide, softly squared head shell with embedded dark screen; two separate round side ear modules.
- Yellow safety hardhat with correctly positioned broad, relatively thin brim. Side/top ridges and green leaf logo; no round clock/gauge replacement for leaf.
- Bright green layered armor **over small recessed dark-green joints**. Distinct shoulder pauldrons, forearm cuffs, chest plate, padded gloves, thigh and shin armor, boot cuffs and oversized rounded boots.
- Limbs short and cute; no long arms/legs or human proportions.
- Back should show a green head and body, not black casings.
- Chest badge: gas gauge with green/yellow/orange/red zones plus leaf motif.
- Reference palette: body #7BC043, dark green #2E6B3F, face #394A41, yellow #FFD42A, LED #B8FF66, orange #FF9A3C, red #E53935.

## Geometry and delivery
- Output an actual textured or material-separated mesh file: GLB preferred, OBJ/FBX acceptable for Blender import.
- Neutral standing / A-pose for easy retopology. Do **not** include props in first master.
- Separate mesh components if supported: HeadShell, FacePanel, HelmetShell, HelmetBrim, HelmetLeaf, BodyArmor, ChestGauge, Shoulders, Arms, Gloves, Legs, Boots.
- Avoid hollow paper-thin cardboards and accidental intersections; use smooth watertight-ish character surfaces where reasonable.
- **Do not generate rigging** before the silhouette and mesh are visually approved.

## Acceptance
Compare front, 3/4, side, and back rendered PNGs against the original sheet at matched framing. Reject or remodel if helmet brim, head shape, glove/boot silhouette, or logo noticeably differs. Treat AI output as a candidate, not a production master. Once approved, refine in Blender, then export `models/Bio_Master.glb`.
